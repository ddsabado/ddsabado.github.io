# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients and collaborators evaluating Dwight Sabado's project work. They need a clear, credible view of the range, usefulness, and execution of showcased projects.

## Product Purpose

A personal project showcase that presents completed work and working demos. It exists to make the quality and breadth of Dwight's problem-solving visible as the portfolio grows beyond its current set of projects.

## Positioning

The portfolio demonstrates practical, end-to-end tools through real project outcomes and live demos. Google Apps Script and Google Workspace automation are a current strength, not a limit on the work that may be showcased.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse featured projects, review project-specific walkthroughs and screenshots, and can open external live demos. Future showcases may use technologies and domains beyond Google Workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A React and Vite web portfolio with a home showcase and project detail routes.
- Current projects include guest registration, personal-finance tracking, and point-of-sale workflows.
- The current interface is a prototype and must not be treated as the established visual standard for the upcoming UI revamp and redesign.
- No specific target industry has been set.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dwight Sabado.
- Present the work as a broad project showcase rather than a portfolio exclusively for Google Apps Script or Google Workspace automation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project copy, linked live demos, and project walkthrough screenshots in `src/assets/`.
- No testimonials, customer claims, benchmarks, pricing, or other proof should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Let real work and demonstrations substantiate capability.
- Make the range of projects easy to understand at a glance.
- Keep the portfolio open to new kinds of work and technology.
- Favor clear project context and outcomes over unsupported claims.
